1. Executive Architectural Summary & Problem Definition

In ophthalmic imaging (fundus photography and OCT), early pathological signs of diabetic retinopathy, glaucoma, and hypertension manifest in micro-capillaries measuring 1 to 2 pixels in width (<50 μm). Mathematically, rapid pixel transitions across 1–2 pixels correspond to high spatial frequencies. Standard spatial denoisers (Gaussian, Bilateral, Median, DnCNN) act as low-pass filters: when removing noise, they indiscriminately attenuate high spatial frequencies, causing artificial vascular dropout (erasing 42.9% of capillaries in empirical tests and mimicking advanced ischemia). SFHformer decouples representation learning into a Spatial Domain Branch (dilated depthwise convolutions preserving edge contours) and a Frequency Domain Branch (2D Real Fast Fourier Transform isolating global periodic noise and scanning lines in O(N log N) complexity).

3. Part II: The SFH Block Micro-Anatomy & Training Engine

  • Local Spatial Mixer: DWConv(k=3, d=1) + DWConv(k=3, d=2) with groups=C. Receptive field spans 3×3 and 5×5 pixels simultaneously without parameter explosion, tracking vessel bifurcations.
  • Global Frequency Mixer (FourierUnit): torch.fft.rfft2 converts tokens to (B, 2C, H, W//2+1). Hermitian symmetry discards redundant negative frequencies. Features pass through BatchNorm, frequency positional encoding (FPE), dynamic complex convolution (FDC via tensor einsum), and inverse transform (irfft2).
  • Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) Fusion: Concatenation → GELU → SE channel attention gating → Conv2d(2C → C, 1×1). Gating shifts weight to the frequency branch in smooth background regions and to the spatial branch at micro-capillaries.
  • Zero-Initialized Scales (β, γ = 0): Fixup initialization guarantees identity mapping at iteration 0, preventing gradient vanishing or explosion during early training.
  • Multi-Scale FFN: Expands to 2C channels across 4 parallel pathways: Identity, DW-Conv 3×3, DW-Conv 5×5, and DW-Conv 7×7, activated by GELU.
  • L1 Loss Formulation: Constant linear gradient (|e|' = ±1) exerts constant gradient pressure on small capillary errors, preventing oversmoothing caused by L2 (MSE) squared penalties.
  • Gradient Accumulation (B=1, N=4): Simulates effective batch size 4 while holding peak VRAM at 4.60 GB on the RTX 5060 Laptop GPU.
  • Windows 11 TDR Mitigation: Cooperative OS sleep yield (time.sleep(0.002)) releases the GPU queue to the Windows Desktop Window Manager after each step, preventing nvlddmkm.sys 2.0s driver timeout Blue Screen crashes (VIDEO_TDR_FAILURE).
