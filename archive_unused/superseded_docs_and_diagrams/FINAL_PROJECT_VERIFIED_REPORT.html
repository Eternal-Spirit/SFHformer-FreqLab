1. Mathematical & Structural Architecture

The model is SFHformer-M (Spatial-Frequency Hybrid Transformer, Jiang et al., ECCV 2024), formulated as a 5-stage hierarchical encoder-decoder U-Net with symmetric skip connections. Key architectural properties:

  • Patch Embedding: Conv2d(3, 32, kernel_size=3, stride=1) with patch size 1 preserves full native pixel resolution from the input layer.
  • Hierarchical Stages: embed_dim = [32, 64, 128, 64, 32] with stage depths [4, 4, 8, 4, 4] (24 total SFH blocks).
  • Dual-Branch SFH Block: Decouples representations into a Local Spatial Mixer (parallel dilated convolutions with d=1 and d=2) and a Global Frequency Mixer (Real 2D FFT rfft2 + complex dynamic convolution fdc via tensor einsum + irfft2), fused via Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) channel attention.
  • Multi-Scale FFN: Expands to 2C channels across 4 parallel pathways: Identity, DW-Conv 3×3, DW-Conv 5×5, and DW-Conv 7×7.
  • Training Engine & Optimization: Trained using L1 loss, AdamW (lr=4e-5, weight decay=1e-4), CosineAnnealingLR schedule, and Gradient Accumulation (effective batch size 4). An explicit cooperative yield (time.sleep(0.002)) prevents the Windows 11 graphics watchdog (nvlddmkm.sys) from triggering a Blue Screen of Death.

2. End-to-End Image Processing, Degradation & Clinical Diagnostics

To ensure clinical reliability, the pipeline couples multi-dataset ingestion with physical degradation modeling and an automated Frangi Hessian vascular biomarker extraction engine:

  • Multi-Dataset Ingestion: 4 international benchmarks (DRIVE, STARE, HRF, APTOS 2019) tiled into 256×256 patches. Patches with <38% retinal tissue are rejected, and remaining crops undergo 8-way orthogonal augmentation (6,620 training patches).
  • Physical Ophthalmic Noise Modeling: Accurately simulates cSLO scanning raster stripes (sinusoidal frequency), laser speckle (multiplicative Gamma), corneal flash glare (annular Gaussian ring), and CMOS shot noise.
  • Adaptive Inference REST Server: Flask server (port 5001) with sub-100ms GPU execution. Combines a Stage 1 Blind FFT Notch Filter for periodic harmonic spikes with Stage 2 SFHformer-M deep restoration.
  • Frangi Hessian Vascular Engine: Isolates the green channel (peak oxygenated hemoglobin optical absorption at 540–575 nm) + CLAHE + multi-scale Frangi Hessian filtering (σ ∈ [1.0, 1.6, 2.2, 2.8, 3.4]) + dynamic Otsu thresholding extracts quantitative Vessel Density %, Dice score, and CNR.
